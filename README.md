## 📖 Overview

**Bookverse** is your ultimate digital library and book-tracking companion. Designed for avid readers, it provides a seamless space to discover, organize, and track your reading journey. Whether you're managing your "To Be Read" (TBR) pile, reviewing your favorite novels, or exploring new genres, Bookverse brings your reading world into one intuitive platform.

## ✨ What It Does

- **📚 Personal Library Management:** Organize your books into custom collections (e.g., Currently Reading, Read, Want to Read, Favorites).
- **🔍 Book Discovery:** Search for books by title, author, or ISBN and instantly fetch details like covers, summaries, and publication info.
- **📈 Reading Progress Tracker:** Log your daily reading progress, set reading goals, and view statistics on your reading habits.
- **✍️ Reviews & Ratings:** Rate the books you've finished and write personal reviews or reading notes.
- **📱 Responsive Design:** Enjoy a beautiful, user-friendly interface that works seamlessly on both desktop and mobile devices.
